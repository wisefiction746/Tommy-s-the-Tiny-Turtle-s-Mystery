    The lantern bearing

    Start where the bridge record says, then apply the three turns in order. Which way do you face at the end?

    Hint

    The Lantern House door

    Arrange the four seals from left to right. Moon comes before Reed. Star sits immediately after Moon. Shell comes after Reed. Read the digits in that order.

      The watchkeeper’s turn log

      At the bridge, the lantern keeper stood facing east. The log lists these turns in order:

      1. Turn right.
      2. Make a U-turn.
      3. Turn left.

      Each turn is relative to the way the keeper is facing at that moment.
